CLAW CONTROL - PLEASE READ

Claw Control is a fast, controller-friendly quick settings panel for the MSI Claw A8. It opens
when you press the MSI Center M button.

MADE ONLY FOR THE MSI CLAW A8
This setup only installs on an MSI Claw A8 (board MS-1T8K). It has been built and tested on
one Claw A8 only. Other Claw models (Claw A1M, Claw 7 AI+, Claw 8 AI+) were not available to the
author, so it has not been tested on them and they are not supported.

WHAT IT DOES TO YOUR SYSTEM
- Installs to C:\Program Files\ClawControl.
- Adds a scheduled task named "Claw Control" that starts it at sign-in with administrator rights
  (needed for power limits and the controller), and restarts it if it ever crashes.
- Takes over the MSI Center M button: MSI's launcher is paused (not removed) while Claw Control
  runs. Quitting or uninstalling hands the button back to MSI.
- When you change something in the panel, it writes it where MSI Center M would: power limits
  (firmware), controller settings (the controller's own memory), AMD graphics settings, display
  and sound settings. Those stay as they are after uninstalling.
- Keeps its settings and a log in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClawControl. It sends nothing anywhere.
